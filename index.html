POur product

Pippy Pets

A pet-care app that takes the paperwork out of health records. Pet parents can say their pet's history out loud, or snap a prescription and let AI read it into the record, down to medicine doses and weight readings. A searchable directory helps them find vets and groomers in Pune.

For businesses: boarders and groomers claim their listing and keep their customer book on their phone. Each pet's food and medication notes arrive before the pet does, and owners get photo updates during a stay.

NHiring platform

Nayi Disha

A WhatsApp-first hiring platform connecting frontline workers in retail and banking with employers.

AI training simulation: staff practise real conversations, such as a customer asking about an education loan, with an AI customer that responds like a real person. A second AI scores the conversation. Then a coach explains what to try differently and sets up a retry.
